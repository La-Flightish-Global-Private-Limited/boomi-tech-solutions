# SEO for LLMs (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, etc.) - Complete Guide

## How LLM Search Works

When users ask ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, or other AI models about companies or services, these systems:

1. **Search the web** using integrated search engines (Bing, Google)
2. **Read your website content** including text, metadata, and structured data
3. **Parse special files** like `llms.txt`, `robots.txt`, sitemap
4. **Analyze context** to understand your business
5. **Generate responses** based on the information found
